Automation

Eliminating manual work at scale — so your teams focus on what truly matters.

Before a single workflow is deployed, I map, challenge, and optimize — ensuring that what gets automated is already working correctly. I specialize in identifying repetitive, low-value tasks and transforming them into intelligent workflows using the right tool for each context: RPA (Automation Anywhere, Blue Prism), Microsoft Power Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps), Python, VBA, and JavaScript. Continuous Improvement
