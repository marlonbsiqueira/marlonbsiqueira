## 👤 About

Finance Transformation Manager with **12+ years of international experience** across multinational SSC/GBS environments in Europe and Latin America. I sit at the intersection of **finance strategy, intelligent automation, and advanced analytics** — architecting the systems and processes that transform how large organisations report, decide, and execute.

At **Stellantis's European SSC/GBS Centre of Excellence**, I lead end-to-end digital finance transformation across 15+ European entities: driving SAP S/4HANA operations across R2R, P2P, and OTC, designing and delivering intelligent automation programmes (RPA, Power Platform, AI), and building executive analytics infrastructure that serves 70+ senior leaders every week.

My hallmark is translating enterprise-scale complexity into **governed, scalable, measurable outcomes** — combining deep technical delivery capability with the stakeholder alignment and programme management skills that make change stick.
